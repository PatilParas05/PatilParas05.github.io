ABOUT ME

Building at the intersection of apps & intelligence.

I'm Paras — an Android and AI/ML engineer who likes taking a fuzzy idea and turning it into something people can actually tap, use, and rely on. On one side I'm writing Kotlin and shipping Android features; on the other I'm training models and figuring out how to get them to run fast, small, and reliably on a phone instead of just in a notebook. I care a lot about the unglamorous parts too — smooth performance, clean state, and code the next person (often future-me) can actually read.

Outside of screens, I'm usually reading about new ML papers I'll probably never fully implement, tinkering with a side project that has no business plan, or trying to explain what I do to my parents in one sentence.
